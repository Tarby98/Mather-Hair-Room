Meet The Team Behind The Chair

Hi, I’m Marcus Mather and welcome to Mather Hair Room. My mother Tracey and I opened the salon in 2024 and we haven’t looked back since. As a NVQ Level 4 Master Barber, I’m passionate about delivering cuts that meet each client’s needs and expectations. For me, barbering is more than just a service — it’s about creating a personal, welcoming experience that every client can enjoy from the moment they walk in. At Mather Hair Room, we take pride in making sure every visit feels tailored, relaxed and memorable.

Hi, I’m Tracey Mather, co-founder of Mather Hair Room. With over 35 years in the hairdressing industry, I’ve built my career around precision, care and a genuine passion for the work I do. I hold NHF NDH Diploma qualifications, along with NVQ Levels 2 & 3 in Hairdressing and NVQ Level 3 in Customer Care. I specialise in colour and perming services, supported by strong product knowledge using Wella, Schwarzkopf and L’Oréal. Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of working alongside incredibly talented stylists, continuously learning and refining my skills throughout my journey.
